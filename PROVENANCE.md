# Provenance

`MatterAtAScale/Basic.lean` and the three files in `FalseControls/` were written for this
repository from *Matter at a Scale: Zero, Span, and the Logarithm Between Them* (August 2026,
v5, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22019892): Eq. (1), Theorems 1 and 2, Lemma 1 and the offset and
span statements of Sec. 3.

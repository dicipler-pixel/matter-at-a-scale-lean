# What is not proved here

Lean proves exactly the statements written, under exactly the hypotheses written.

* The units dictionary is proved as exact algebra of power laws in `s = 1 + z`; the physical
  claim that redshift is atomic, and the fork between reframing and environmental scaling
  (Sec. 2.3), are not formalized.
* Lemma 1 is proved for eigenspaces of a linear map. Principal angles, Riesz projectors of
  non-diagonalisable operators and the boundary census are not formalized.
* Theorem 3 (infrared span invariance), the ultraviolet logarithm and its coefficient
  `A = c/π²`, the Majorana `c = 1/2` test, the linearity law `A = 2c/π²`, the matter/gas
  classification (Sec. 4) and the SPARC confrontation (Sec. 5) are measured or analytic results,
  not Lean proofs.
